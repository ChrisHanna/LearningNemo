merely appearing uncertain due to the browser. The recent run records have
`Calls = 0`, and authority was revoked well before its expiry.

For job `b87b61e43d364e54a6d2f9bc57c22c40`:

| 18:21:46.706 | sandbox-bound |

There are no agent-runtime events in this run's five recorded activities, and
the run made no admitted SQL-backed agent/tool calls. This narrows the observed
failure to the launch/execution interval after authority issuance; it does not
identify the exact underlying exception. It is not evidence that tables or
procedures are missing, that SQL is paused, or that sandbox capacity is full.

`InvoiceJobs.work` catches general exceptions and stores a generic unconfirmed
result rather than the exception. That prevents the persisted job result alone
from explaining the launch failure. The next diagnostic step is to inspect the
retained host launch error/exit evidence and surface a sanitized internal error
correlation without replaying the job.
